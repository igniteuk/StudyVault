StudyVault privacy policy

Your subjects, flashcards and review progress are stored in your browser. You can export and import a local backup from Settings. Browser site controls let you remove local data.

Optional Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive and choose Sync now, StudyVault uploads study data to the private app data folder in the Google account you select. StudyVault requests access to its app data folder and uses the data for backup and cross-device sync.

StudyVault keeps its short-lived Google access token in memory while Settings is open. Disconnecting clears the local session. You can remove StudyVault’s permission through your Google Account settings and remove its hidden app data in Drive’s app management settings.

StudyVault’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
